## Overview

**StudentHub** is a centralized student portal designed to simplify and organize the academic and campus experience in one place.

The platform provides students with quick access to essential services such as **courses, timetables, attendance, grades, assignments, events, announcements, learning resources, and communication**.

StudentHub is designed with a **clean, intuitive, and accessible interface**, making frequently used academic information easy to find while providing a structured foundation for future features and backend integration.

### What StudentHub Provides

- **Academic Management** — Courses, timetable, attendance, grades and assignments
- **Campus Updates** — Events, announcements and important notifications
- **Learning Resources** — Study materials and digital resources
- **Student Profile** — Personal and academic information
- **Communication** — Department contacts, support and feedback
- **Administration** — User, course, content and report management

> **StudentHub — One platform. Everything students need.**
