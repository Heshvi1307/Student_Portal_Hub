Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for StudentHub?

Go to the Register page and enter your personal, academic and account information.

How do I login?

Open the Login page and enter your university email and password.

What can I see on the dashboard?

The dashboard displays your schedule, attendance, academic progress and notifications.

How can I contact student services?

Visit the Contact page and use the contact form to send a message.

Where can I find university events?

Visit the Events page to view upcoming and past events.
